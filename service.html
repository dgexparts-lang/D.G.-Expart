Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services.

Which services does D.G. EXPART provide? +
We provide digital marketing, social media management, website development, SEO, AI search visibility, Google Business Profile management, video editing, paid advertising, AI automation, and branding services.
Can I choose only one service? +
Absolutely. You can pick individual services based on your current needs. We'll help you decide which combination works best for your business goals.
Do you create websites for local businesses? +
Yes. We specialize in websites for local businesses, shops, coaching institutes, salons, gyms, and professional service providers in Meerut and nearby areas.
Do website charges include domain and hosting? +
Domain and hosting are typically charged separately at actual cost. We'll clearly list these in your quotation before starting any work. Some packages may include first-year hosting.
Is advertising spend included in campaign management? +
No. Our service fee covers campaign setup, planning, and management. The actual ad spend (Google Ads, Meta Ads budget) is paid directly to the ad platform and is separate.
Can you manage social media accounts? +
Yes. We handle content planning, design, captions, hashtags, posting schedules, and monthly performance reporting for Instagram, Facebook, and other platforms.
Do you offer AI chatbot and automation solutions? +
Yes. We build website chatbots, WhatsApp automation flows, lead capture workflows, FAQ automation, and AI-assisted customer support tools. Third-party subscription costs may apply separately.
How can I request a customized package? +
Simply message us on WhatsApp at +91 95280 95030 or fill the contact form. We'll understand your business, discuss your goals, and prepare a custom plan within 1-2 days.
How do I get started? +
Click "Get Free Consultation" anywhere on this page. We'll schedule a free 30-minute call to discuss your business and recommend the best path forward — no obligation.
