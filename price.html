Pricing FAQs

Common questions about our pricing and payment.

Is GST included in the prices? +
No, all prices are exclusive of GST (18%). GST will be added to the final invoice. Ad spend (Google/Meta Ads budget) is separate and paid directly to platforms.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI, bank transfer, credit/debit cards, and cash. For monthly packages, payments are collected at the start of each billing cycle.
Do you offer custom packages? +
Absolutely! If our standard packages don't fit your needs, we'll build a custom package tailored to your business goals and budget. Contact us on WhatsApp to discuss.
Can I switch packages later? +
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your package at any time. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle. No long-term contracts required.
Is there any setup fee? +
No hidden setup fees. The price you see is what you pay for the service (excluding GST & ad spend).
